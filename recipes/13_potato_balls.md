### Potato Balls

Deep-fried potato balls

### PREPARATION
- 1   In a large pot, boil the potatoes for about 30 min or until cooked.
- 2   Drain then allow to cool. Peel and discard the skin, then mash.
- 3   Mix in the egg white and salt.
- 4   Form the mixture into balls 3 cm in diameter.

### METHOD

- 1   Heat some oil in a wok or pan over a medium-high flame.
- 2   Deep-fry the potato balls until light golden brown. Remove and drain well.
- 3   Place on a serving plate. Serve with chilli sauce and/or ketchup on the side.
