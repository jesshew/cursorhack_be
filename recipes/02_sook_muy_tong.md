## Sook Muy Tong

Cream corn and minced chicken soup

This is a very easy soup to prepare since the ingredients are easy to obtain and inexpensive. I find that kids love it because it's tasty, easy to eat and bright yellow in colour.

### INGREDIENTS

### PREPARATION
300 g minced chicken

1 /2 tbsp Chinese cooking wine or sherry

1 tsp salt

a dash of pepper

4 tbsp corn flour (cornstarch)

1 l chicken stock

2 eggs

425 g (1 can) cream corn

### Tips:

- ·   For a healthier version, prepare the minced chicken by using skinless chicken breasts then finely mincing it rather than buying ready-minced chicken.
- ·   Using homemade chicken stock is also preferable. Alternatively, high quality, organic and MSG-free chicken stock is available in some stores.
- ·   Although the recipe specifies using 1 can of cream corn, you can use less or more according to your preference.
- 1   In a bowl with the minced chicken, add the wine or sherry, salt and pepper.
- 2   Dissolve the corn flour in 5 tbsp chicken stock, then add it to the minced chicken mixture. Combine.
- 3   Beat the eggs then set aside.

### METHOD

- 1   In a large pot, bring the remaining chicken stock and cream corn to the boil.
- 2   Add the chicken mixture. Stir continually (to prevent it from clumping) while simmering for 3-5 min.
- 3   T urn off the flame then pour the beaten egg into the hot soup while stirring (which will result in a feathered effect). The residual heat in the soup will cook the egg.
- 4   Garnish with chopped spring onion or coriander (optional).
